Hobbies

Life outside the code

When I'm not studying or working on software, I spend my time on things that keep me both creative and grounded. Interests outside of technology matter — not only for balance, but for inspiration and long-term motivation.

Gaming

Strategy, teamwork and leadership

A long-standing hobby that has shaped how I think about strategy and teamwork. For three years I ran a World of Warcraft guild and led its 25-player raid team at the highest difficulty — a role I grew into because I enjoyed getting a big group to work well together.

Programming

Code just for fun

Not everything I write needs a purpose. I solve algorithm puzzles on Kattis and tinker with half-baked ideas just to see if they work — the same curiosity that got me into software in the first place.

Basketball & fitness

Focus, energy and balance

Playing basketball and working out keeps me healthy and clears my head — especially during busy periods of study or work.

Music

Listening and creating

A creative outlet that contrasts nicely with the structured nature of programming, and a big part of how I relax and recharge.

Family & friends

The people who matter most

I live with my partner and our young son, who has added a new and meaningful dimension to everyday life. Balancing family, hobbies and professional growth is something I care deeply about.
