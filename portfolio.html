How I work

Building blocks, not finished products

These projects represent different stages of my learning journey. Each one taught me something new — and I keep revisiting older projects to refine them as my skills develop.

From idea to completion

I enjoy small, focused projects where I can take an idea all the way — planning structure, designing simple interfaces and refining functionality through testing and iteration.

Front-end and beyond

From responsive layouts in HTML, CSS and JavaScript to application logic in Java and JavaFX. Even in small projects I put emphasis on readability, structure and maintainability.

Full-stack in practice

With Tackly I've gone full-stack — mobile app, database, security rules and admin tooling. I'm open to small tasks, prototypes and proof-of-concepts for startups, smaller companies and private clients.

Let's build something together

Interested in collaboration, feedback, or just want to discuss an idea? I'm always eager to learn, contribute and grow through hands-on, real-world challenges.
