About me

Curious, hands-on and calm under pressure.

I study Software Engineering on VIA University College's flexible engineering trainee programme. The teaching is online, which means I can work around two days a week in a company and write my semester and bachelor projects directly in its technologies — creating long-term value for both the company and myself.

I pick up software architecture, databases and full-stack development quickly, but my biggest strength is bridging the gap between the technology and the end user. I always dig into the fundamentals to understand why systems work and what value they bring — which is also what helps me learn new languages and frameworks fast.

I'm on my 3rd semester now, learning C# and .NET, building a CPU from logic gates in DigiSim, and digging into algorithms and time complexity.

Every semester gives me new tools — and I love taking my time to put them to work in side projects, big and small. On the small side: a clean one-pager for my good friend's barbershop in Roskilde, Hos Qilej. On the big side: Tackly, a marketplace app for equestrian equipment that a friend and I are about to publish on Google Play — with the App Store as the next stop. Need a website or a small app built? Feel free to reach out.

On our 2nd-semester project, LearnHub, I was Product Owner — keeping the overview, the requirements and the progress on track, with great feedback from fellow students and teachers. I quickly came to really enjoy the role, and I'd like to grow in that direction: I thrive in the space between code and development on one side, and customers, their needs and wishes on the other.

Before my studies I worked as a technical customer service agent for PayPal in Belfast, served as a firefighter and rescue specialist with the Danish Emergency Management Agency, and spent several years in healthcare and the service sector — including data registration at Roskilde Hospital's Department of Hematology. That taught me to stay calm, take ownership and explain complex things in a language everyone understands.

I'm fluent in Danish and English, speak Spanish and Norwegian well, understand some German, and would welcome an international or English-speaking work environment.

A little more personal

Outside of studies and work I enjoy gaming, basketball, and music — both listening to it and making it. I value balance between professional ambition and personal life, and see it as an important part of long-term success.
