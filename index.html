01About me

Curious, hands-on and calm under pressure.

I've always wanted to know why things work — and once I know, I can't stop asking myself whether they could work better. Is this really the best it can be? Where's the bottleneck? What would make it smoother for everyone using it? Software is where that curiosity and creativity finally gets to build something. I enjoy investigating the underlying reasons, and the habit goes way back: I spent more hours than I'd like to admit theorycrafting gear and builds in games, just to squeeze out more damage. To me, every technical challenge has an explanation, and uncovering it is what drives my curiosity as a developer.

I'm on my 3rd semester now, learning C# and .NET, building a CPU from logic gates in DigiSim, and digging into algorithms and time complexity.

Every semester hands me new tools, and I can't resist putting them to work outside class too. That's taken me from a clean one-pager for my good friend's barbershop in Roskilde, Hos Qilej, all the way to Tackly — a full marketplace app for equestrian equipment that a friend and I just launched on Google Play, with the App Store up next. Websites, apps and everything in between — I love the variety. Need a website or a small app built? Feel free to reach out!

On our 2nd-semester project, LearnHub, I was Product Owner, keeping the overview, the requirements and the progress on track. Organising people wasn't completely new to me (a few years of leading 25-man raid nights will do that), but this was the first time I got to do it with code, and I loved it. It's a direction I want to keep growing in.

I thrive in the space between the code and the people it's built for.

Rescue specialist training and international tech support taught me to stay calm, take ownership and explain complex things so anyone can follow. Add fluent English, and I'd feel right at home in an international or English-speaking workplace.

A little more personal

Home is Hedehusene, with my long-time partner and our young son. I care a lot about balancing ambition with the people who matter — here's what I get up to off the school bench.
